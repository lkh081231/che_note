observation 一列是 mark scheme 的标准写法 —— 考试要的是现象， 不是「某某类别 positive」。

reagentpositive observation 测什么判分要点
acidified K₂Cr₂O₇(aq) (solution) turns (from orange to) green 1°/2° alcohol · aldehyde BOD green 写成 blue/grey/brown（dichromate 过量）；3° alcohol remains orange
alkaline I₂(aq) yellow precipitate (forms) CH₃CH(OH)–R 或 CH₃CO–R A aqueous iodine / iodine water，不能只写 iodine；R iodoform 作试剂名
2,4-DNPH orange precipitate (forms) aldehyde 与 ketone 皆 positive A red / orange / yellow ppt
Tollens' reagent silver mirror · grey ppt · black ppt 只有 aldehyde positive ketone 呈 no reaction / no observable change
Fehling's reagent blue solution → red ppt 只有 aldehyde positive negative 写 (仍为) orange solution / no ppt；R 说 ketone 也被 Fehling's oxidised
Br₂(aq) bromine water orange / brown to colourless C=C A decolourises · A yellow to colourless · R red
AgNO₃(aq) in ethanol Cl⁻ white · Br⁻ cream / off-white · I⁻ yellow ppt halogenoalkane 里的 X hydrolysis rate I > Br > Cl（C–X bond 往下越弱）
Na(s) effervescence / fizzing / bubbling –OH 与 –COOH 皆 positive I 只写「产生 gas」；I gas 名称或检验方法；I 「solid 消失」
Na₂CO₃(aq) fizzing / effervescence 只有 carboxylic acid A bubbles；I limewater (turns milky)；alcohol / phenol acidity 不足，不反应
PCl₅(s) steamy / misty fumes –OH 与 –COOH alkene no reaction，记 ×
