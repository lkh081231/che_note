Chlorine is one of the elements in Group 17 of the Periodic Table.

### (a)(i) [1] · ch11

Describe the colours of the Group 17 elements, chlorine to iodine, at room temperature.

**答案**
- chlorine = yellow-green
- bromine = orange / brown / red
- iodine = silver grey / black

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch11

Describe the relative reactivity of the elements chlorine to iodine as oxidising agents.

**答案** — oxidising strength **decreases** from chlorine to iodine

### (a)(iii) [1] · ch11

State what is observed when chlorine reacts with hydrogen.

**答案** — green colour disappears

### (a)(iv) [1] · ch11

Explain why the thermal stability of the hydrogen halides decreases down the group.

**答案** — H—Hal covalent bond strength **decreases** down the group

### (b) The halogenoalkane CH₃CH₂Cl forms when chlorine reacts with C₂H₆ via a free-radical substitution mechanism.

### (b)(i) [1] · ch13

Define free radical.

**答案** — a species with one or more unpaired electrons

### (b)(ii) [1] · ch14

State the essential condition for chlorine to react with C₂H₆ at room temperature.

**答案** — ultraviolet (light)

### (b)(iii) [2] · ch14

Write two equations to show the propagation steps in this reaction.

**答案**
- C₂H₆ + Cl• → C₂H₅• + HCl
- C₂H₅• + Cl₂ → C₂H₅Cl + Cl•

### (c) CHCl₃ is another halogenoalkane. CHCl₃ forms when propanone reacts with NaClO. NaClO is made from chlorine in a disproportionation reaction.

### (c)(i) [1] · ch11

Identify a reagent and conditions that can be used to convert chlorine to NaClO.

**答案** — cold NaOH(aq)

### (c)(ii) [1] · ch6

Define disproportionation.

**答案** — a reaction where a species / same species is both oxidised and reduced simultaneously / in the same reaction

### (c)(iii) [1] · ch2

Write numbers in the boxes to balance the equation showing the reaction of propanone with NaClO.

**答案** — CH₃COCH₃ + **3**NaClO → **1**CHCl₃ + **1**CH₃COONa + **2**NaOH

### (c)(iv) [2] · ch15

Aqueous AgNO₃ dissolved in ethanol reacts with an aqueous solution of CHCl₃. State what is observed in this reaction. Explain your answer.

**答案**
- M1 white precipitate / white ppt forms
- M2 CHCl₃ is hydrolysed / chloride ions are released
- OR Cl⁻ / chloride ions which are released react with Ag⁺
- OR silver chloride / AgCl is formed
