## Q1 [20]　(兼 ch7 Equilibria)

Chromium, Cr, and its compounds are widely used in many chemical reactions.

### (a) Cr exists as four stable isotopes.

### (a)(i) [1] · ch1

The most common isotope of Cr is chromium-52. Determine the number of protons, neutrons and
electrons in an atom of chromium-52.

**答案** — number of protons = 24；neutrons = 28；electrons = 24

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch1

Describe how an atom of chromium-54 differs from an atom of chromium-52. Refer to numbers of
particles in your answer.

**答案** — (⁵⁴Cr has two) more neutrons (than ⁵²Cr)

### (a)(iii) [1] · ch22

The relative isotopic masses of the isotopes of Cr can be determined using mass spectrometry.
State what other information is needed to calculate the relative atomic mass, *A*ᵣ, of Cr.

**答案** — (relative) abundance (of each isotope)

### (a)(iv) [1] · ch22

Atoms of ⁵²Cr, ⁵³Cr and ⁵⁴Cr make up more than 95% of naturally occurring chromium atoms. The *A*ᵣ
of naturally occurring Cr is 51.996. Suggest what these statements imply about the relative
isotopic mass of the fourth stable isotope of chromium.

**答案** — the fourth isotope has any value of RIM less than 51.996

### (b) The shorthand electronic configuration of chromium is [Ar] 3d⁵4s¹.

### (b)(i) [1] · ch1

Complete the full electronic configuration of chromium.

**答案** — 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ (3d⁵ 4s¹)

### (b)(ii) [1] · ch1

Deduce the total number of unpaired electrons in an atom of chromium.

**答案** — 6

### (c)(iv) [2] · ch3

Methanal is a liquid at −30 °C but carbon dioxide is a gas at this temperature. Explain why.

**答案**
- M1 CO₂ has only instantaneous/temporary dipole–induced dipole forces **AND** methanal has
  permanent dipole–permanent dipole (and id-id) forces
- M2 IMF's in methanal are stronger than IMF's in CO₂

### (d) In acidic conditions, a dynamic equilibrium is established between CrO₄²⁻(aq) and Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq).

2CrO₄²⁻(aq) + 2H⁺(aq) ⇌ Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq) + H₂O(l)　Δ*H* > 0

### (d)(i) [1] · ch7

State what is meant by dynamic equilibrium.

**答案** — rate of forward and backward (reaction) are the same/equal　**OR** concentrations of
reactants and products are constant

### (d)(ii) [1] · ch7

Identify the condition necessary to establish dynamic equilibrium.

**答案** — closed system

### (d)(iii) [4] · ch7

CrO₄²⁻(aq) ions are yellow and Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq) ions are orange. State what is observed when the
following changes are made to an equilibrium mixture of acidified CrO₄²⁻(aq) and Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq)
ions. Explain your answers.

- The equilibrium mixture is warmed gently.
- Dilute HCl(aq) is added to the equilibrium mixture.

**答案**
- M1 (mixture becomes more) orange
- M2 forward reaction is endothermic
- M3 (mixture becomes more) orange
- M4 equilibrium moves (forwards/right) to compensate for extra acid / H⁺

### (e) [2] · ch9

Chromium(IV) fluoride, CrF₄, is a covalent molecule that shows similar chemical properties to
SiCl₄. Suggest the type of reaction that occurs when CrF₄ is placed in water. Construct a relevant
equation for this reaction.

**答案**
- type of reaction — hydrolysis
- equation — CrF₄ + 2H₂O → CrO₂ + 4HF
