## Q1 [10]

Manganese, Mn, and its compounds are widely used in many chemical reactions.

### (a) Mn is usually found as a single isotope, manganese-55.

### (a)(i) [1]

Determine the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in an atom of manganese-55.

**答案** — number of protons = 25；neutrons = 30；electrons = 25

### (a)(ii) [1]

Define isotopes.

**答案** — (atoms of an element) with the same number of protons and different number of neutrons

### (a)(iii) [2]

A sample of manganese from the Moon is found to contain manganese-53 in addition to
manganese-55. State the two pieces of information needed to determine the relative atomic mass,
*A*ᵣ, of manganese in this sample.

**答案**
- M1 (relative) isotopic mass　**OR** (relative isotopic) mass of Mn-55 and Mn-53
- M2 (relative) abundance of each isotope

### (b) The shorthand electronic configuration of manganese is [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s².

### (b)(i) [1]

Complete the full electronic configuration of manganese.

**答案** — 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ (3d⁵ 4s²)

### (b)(ii) [1]

Deduce the total number of unpaired electrons in an atom of manganese.

**答案** — 5

### (c) Manganese(IV) oxide reacts with methanal, CH₂O, in acidic conditions to produce carbon dioxide. The movement of electrons to or from relevant species is shown in the following half-equations.

half-equation 1　CH₂O + H₂O → CO₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻

half-equation 2　MnO₂ + 4H⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 2H₂O

### (c)(i) [1]

Identify the species that is reduced in half-equation 2. Explain your answer.

**答案** — Mn / MnO₂ **AND** it gains electrons

### (c)(ii) [1]

The oxidation state of carbon in methanal is 0. Calculate the oxidation state of carbon in
carbon dioxide.

**答案** — (+)4
