The Group 2 elements Mg to Ba show trends in their physical properties down the group.

### (a) Table 1.1 shows some information about the elements Mg to Ba.

**Table 1.1**

| element | proton number | shorthand electronic configuration | sum of first and second ionisation energies / kJ mol⁻¹ | third ionisation energy / kJ mol⁻¹ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mg | 12 | [Ne] 3s² | 2186 | 7740 |
| Ca | 20 | [Ar] 4s² | 1740 | 4940 |
| Sr | 38 | [Kr] 5s² | 1608 | 4120 |
| Ba | 56 | [Xe] 6s² | 1468 | 3390 |

### (a)(i) [1] · ch1

Give the full electronic configuration of a calcium atom.

**答案** — 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 4s²

### (a)(ii) [2] · ch1

Explain the trend shown in the sum of the first and second ionisation energies, as proton
number increases.

**答案** — (ionisation energy decreases because of) more shells / larger (atomic/ionic) radius;
increased shielding; less attraction (for the nucleus/protons) / lower energy required (to
remove electrons)

### (a)(iii) [1] · ch1

Write an equation to represent the third ionisation energy of barium.

**答案** — Ba²⁺(g) → Ba³⁺(g) + e⁻

### (a)(iv) [2] · ch1

Explain why the third ionisation energy of magnesium is much greater than the sum of its first
and second ionisation energies.

**答案**
- M1 electron removed from closer / inner / lower-energy (p sub)shell/orbital
- M2 (much) less shielding OR greater nuclear attraction

### (b) When strontium reacts with water, an alkaline solution is produced.

### (b)(i) [2] · ch10

Write an equation for this reaction. Include state symbols.

**答案** — M1 Sr(s) + 2H₂O(l) → Sr(OH)₂(aq) + H₂(g)；M2 state symbols for all species

### (b)(ii) [2] · ch10

Samples of the alkaline solution produced in (b)(i) are tested with dilute HCl(aq) and
separately with dilute H₂SO₄(aq).

State what you would observe in each test.

**答案** — with HCl(aq)：no visible reaction / no change (observed)；with H₂SO₄(aq)：white
precipitate/solid forms

### (c) The nitrates and carbonates of Group 2 elements decompose on heating.

### (c)(i) [1] · ch10

State the trend in the thermal stability of the Group 2 carbonates down the group.

**答案** — (thermal stability) increases (down the group)

### (c)(ii) [1] · ch10

Write an equation for the thermal decomposition of Ca(NO₃)₂.

**答案** — 2Ca(NO₃)₂ → 2CaO + 4NO₂ + O₂

### (d) Calcium chlorate(I), Ca(ClO)₂, is used for water purification in the same way as sodium chlorate(I), NaClO. Ca(ClO)₂ can be prepared by the reaction of aqueous Ca(OH)₂ with Cl₂.

### (d)(i) [1] · ch11

Write an ionic equation for the reaction of cold dilute OH⁻ ions with Cl₂.

**答案** — 2OH⁻ + Cl₂ → ClO⁻ + Cl⁻ + H₂O

### (d)(ii) [1] · ch11

State why NaClO and Ca(ClO)₂ are used in water purification.

**答案** — (ClO⁻) kills bacteria
