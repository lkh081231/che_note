## Q3 [10]　⟨镜像 · 主章 ch11 Group 17⟩

> 本题正本在 [`ch11-group-17/9701_s26_21.md`](../ch11-group-17/9701_s26_21.md)，此处为镜像。**不要在本文件里改它** —— 改正本，再跑 `python tools/mirror.py`。

Separate samples of NaCl, NaBr and NaI each react with concentrated H₂SO₄. Table 3.1 shows the observations for each reaction.

**Table 3.1**

| reaction | compound | observations |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NaCl | effervescence; steamy fumes that turn blue litmus paper red |
| 2 | NaBr | effervescence; steamy fumes initially that turn blue litmus paper red; brown vapour; pungent smell of SO₂ |
| 3 | NaI | effervescence; steamy fumes initially that turn blue litmus paper red; purple vapour; pungent smell of SO₂ and then a rotten egg smell of H₂S |

### (a)(i) [1] · ch11

Write an equation for reaction 1.

**答案** — H₂SO₄ + NaCl → NaHSO₄ + HCl
**判分** — A H₂SO₄ + 2NaCl → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl；A Cl⁻ + H₂SO₄ → HCl + HSO₄⁻；A 2Cl⁻ + H₂SO₄ → 2HCl + SO₄²⁻

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch7

Identify the reactant that behaves as a Brønsted–Lowry base in reaction 1.

**答案** — Cl⁻ / NaCl
**判分** — A sodium chloride / chloride (ion)；A chlorine anion；**I** chlorine ion；**I** chlorine；**R** Cl₂

### (b) [1] · ch11

State the formulae of the species responsible for the brown vapour in reaction 2 and for the purple vapour in reaction 3.

**答案**
- brown vapour……Br₂
- purple vapour……I₂

**判分** — **I** bromine / **I** Br；**I** iodine / **I** I；**R** iodide / bromide

### (c) [1] · ch11

Explain why reaction 3 produces purple vapour.

**答案** — (purple vapour is produced when) HI / iodide (anions) are oxidised (by H₂SO₄)
OR (sulfuric) acid / H₂SO₄ oxidises I⁻ (to iodine)

**判分** — A iodide / I⁻ loses electrons；A H₂SO₄ / S (in H₂SO₄) is reduced by HI / I⁻ / I in NaI / I in HI；A I⁻ oxidised by S(VI) (in H₂SO₄)；**I** displacement / oxidation products / oxidation number（除非明确指 S(VI) in H₂SO₄）；**I** "反应放热所以碘被蒸发" 这类解释；**R** iodine is oxidised by sulfuric acid

### (d) [2] · ch6

Complete Table 3.2.

**Table 3.2**

| species | oxidation number of sulfur |
|---|---|
| H₂SO₄ | (+)VI OR (+)6 |
| SO₂ | (+)IV OR (+)4 |
| H₂S | −II OR −2 |

**判分** — A 6(+) 4(+) and 2−；A VI(+) AND IV(+) AND II−；**R** S⁺⁴ S⁺⁶ or S⁻² / S⁴⁺ S⁶⁺ or S²⁻ 这类写法

### (e) [1] · ch11

Explain why reaction 3 produces H₂S but reaction 2 does not produce H₂S.

**答案** — iodide / I⁻ is a stronger reducing agent (than bromide / Br⁻)
OR HBr / bromide / Br⁻ is a weaker reducing agent (than iodide / I⁻)
OR HBr / Bromide / Br⁻ can't reduce S(IV) / SO₂

**判分** — A the reducing ability of the halides increases down the group；**I** bond energy / reactivity（未联系 redox）/ electronegativity 论证；**I** references to H₂SO₄ being an oxidising agent

### (f)(i) [2] · ch11

Describe what is observed when Ag⁺(aq), followed by NH₃(aq), are added to a sample of NaI(aq).

**答案**
- observation after addition of Ag⁺(aq)……(pale) yellow precipitate
- observation after addition of NH₃(aq)……precipitate remains / precipitate does not dissolve / precipitate does not disappear

**判分** — precipitate = ppt(e) = crystals = powder = (insoluble) solid；M1 ignore shades，**R** any other colour；M2 A no change / no reaction / no visible change（若 M1 已见 ppt）；M2 A precipitate is insoluble；M2 A ecf ppt remains on incorrect colour ppt in M1

### (f)(ii) [1] · ch11

Write an ionic equation for the reaction of Ag⁺(aq) with NaI(aq).

**答案** — Ag⁺ + I⁻ → AgI
**判分** — **I** Ag⁺ + NaI → AgI + Na⁺；**I** Ag⁺ + Na⁺ + I⁻ → Ag⁺ + I⁻ + Na⁺
