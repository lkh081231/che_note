### (a)(i) [1] · ch18 ch21

Propanoic acid, CH₃CH₂COOH, reacts with reducing agent Q to produce propan-1-ol,
CH₃CH₂CH₂OH. Suggest the formula of reducing agent Q.

**答案** — LiAlH₄

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch18 ch21

Complete the equation to show the reduction of propanoic acid to propan-1-ol. Use [H] to
represent one atom of hydrogen from Q.

……CH₃CH₂COOH + ……[H] →

**答案** — CH₃CH₂COOH + 4[H] → CH₃CH₂CH₂OH + H₂O

### (b)(i) [1] · ch13 ch21

Propan-1-ol is converted to compound T in a three-step synthesis. In step 1, SOCl₂ is added to
propan-1-ol to produce compound E. Name the type of reaction that occurs in step 1.

**答案** — substitution

### (b)(ii) [1] · ch15 ch21

In step 2, E reacts with a suitable reagent to produce butanenitrile. Identify the reagent and
conditions required in step 2.

**答案** — KCN / potassium cyanide in ethanol / C₂H₅OH **AND** heat

### (b)(iii) [2] · ch19 ch21

In step 3, butanenitrile is heated with NaOH(aq). Construct an equation to describe the reaction
of butanenitrile with NaOH(aq) in step 3.

**答案**
- CH₃CH₂CH₂CN + NaOH + H₂O → CH₃CH₂CH₂CO₂Na + NH₃
- OR CH₃CH₂CH₂CN + NaOH + 2H₂O → CH₃CH₂CH₂CO₂Na + NH₄OH

**判分** — M1 correct organic product (as molecule or anion)；M2 rest of equation is correct

### (b)(iv) [1] · ch13 ch21

Name the type of reaction that occurs in step 3.

**答案** — (alkaline) hydrolysis

### (c)(i) [1] · ch13

Compounds A, B and C belong to the alcohol homologous series. Each molecule of A, B and C
contains four saturated carbon atoms. Identify the type of hybridisation shown in the saturated
carbon atoms of all alcohols.

**答案** — sp³

### (c)(ii) [1] · ch16

Identify the gas produced when Na(s) is added to separate samples of each alcohol.

**答案** — hydrogen / H₂

### (c)(iii) [1] · ch16

Describe the role of Na(s) when it reacts with alcohols.

**答案** — (It / Na) reduces A / B / C / the alcohol (group) OR (it / Na behaves as) reducing agent
OR (it / Na) donates electrons to A / B / C / the alcohol (group)

### (d)(i) [1] · ch16

Table 4.1 shows the results of two tests on separate samples of A, B and C. Deduce the structure
of A.

**Table 4.1**

| compound | heat under reflux with acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ | warm with alkaline I₂(aq) |
|---|---|---|
| A | remains orange | no visible change |
| B | orange to green | no visible change |
| C | orange to green | pale yellow precipitate |

**答案** — A = (CH₃)₃COH（2-methylpropan-2-ol，tertiary alcohol，不被 K₂Cr₂O₇ 氧化）

### (d)(ii) [1] · ch16

Deduce a possible name for B.

**答案** — butan-1-ol OR (2-)methylpropan-1-ol

### (d)(iii) [1] · ch16

Deduce the identity of C.

**答案** — butan-2-ol OR CH₃CH₂CH(OH)CH₃
