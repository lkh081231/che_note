### (a) Diamond and graphite are both crystalline solids made from carbon atoms. Graphite conducts electricity. Diamond does not conduct electricity.

### (a)(i) [1] · ch4

Name the type of lattice structure present in the crystalline solids diamond and graphite.

**答案** — (both) giant molecular

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch4

Explain how graphite conducts electricity.

**答案** — delocalised electrons move through the structure　**OR** delocalised electrons move
through / between the layers

### (a)(iii) [1] · ch4

Explain why diamond does not conduct electricity.

**答案** — no delocalised electrons (to carry charge through the structure)　**OR** all (4) outer
electrons are used in bonding

### (b) Separate samples of phosphorus(V) chloride and silicon(IV) chloride are each added to an excess of cold water.

### (b)(i) [2] · ch9

Write an equation for each reaction.

**答案**
- PCl₅ + 4H₂O → H₃PO₄ + 5HCl
- SiCl₄ + 2H₂O → SiO₂ + 4HCl

### (b)(ii) [2] · ch9

Describe the appearance of phosphorus(V) chloride and silicon(IV) chloride at room temperature.

**答案**
- phosphorus(V) chloride：white / yellow solid
- silicon(IV) chloride：colourless liquid

### (b)(iii) [2] · ch9

Compare the appearance of the mixtures produced when each reaction is complete.

**答案**
- M1（phosphorus(V) chloride）produces colourless solution
- M2 silicon(IV) chloride produces white suspension / white precipitate (in a colourless liquid /
  mixture / solution)

### (c) Some oxides are amphoteric.

### (c)(i) [1] · ch9

Describe what is meant by amphoteric.

**答案** — species behaves as acid and base　**OR** species reacts with (both) acid and base

### (c)(ii) [1] · ch9

Identify the formula of a Period 3 oxide that is amphoteric.

**答案** — Al₂O₃
