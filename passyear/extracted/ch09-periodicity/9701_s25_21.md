### (a) Solid sodium conducts electricity. Sodium oxide conducts electricity when molten but not when solid.

### (a)(i) [1] · ch3

Name the type of bonding present in sodium and in sodium oxide.

**答案** — sodium – metallic　**AND** sodium oxide – ionic

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch4

Explain how solid sodium conducts electricity.

**答案** — delocalised electrons move (through the structure)

### (a)(iii) [1] · ch4

Explain why sodium oxide conducts electricity when molten.

**答案** — (sodium and oxide) ions move (through the liquid)

### (b) Separate samples of sodium and sodium oxide are each added to an excess of cold water.

### (b)(i) [1] · ch9

Write an equation for the reaction of sodium with cold water.

**答案** — 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂

### (b)(ii) [1] · ch9

Write an equation for the reaction of sodium oxide with cold water.

**答案** — Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH

### (c) [2] · ch9

State the oxidation number of the Period 3 elements bonded to Cl in NaCl and PCl₅. Explain the
difference in the oxidation number.
