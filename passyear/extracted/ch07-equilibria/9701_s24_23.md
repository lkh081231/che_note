## Q2 [14]

N₂(g) reacts with H₂(g) in the Haber process, as shown in reaction 1.

reaction 1　N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)　　Δ*H* = –x kJ mol⁻¹

Table 2.1 shows the different conditions used to produce three equilibrium mixtures, A, B and C.

**Table 2.1**

| | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial molar ratio of N₂ : H₂ added | 1 : 3 | 1 : 3 | 1 : 3 |
| temperature / °C | 500 | 500 | 1000 |
| pressure / atm | 1000 | 1000 | 1000 |
| iron present in mixture | no | yes | no |
| percentage yield of NH₃(g) at equilibrium | 58 | x | y |

### (a) [1]

Describe and explain the change, if any, to the percentage yield of NH₃(g) produced in B
compared to A.

**答案** — 58% / no change / stays the same AND catalysts don't affect yield / iron is a catalyst

### (b)(i) [1]

Describe and explain the change, if any, to the percentage yield of NH₃(g) produced in C
compared to A.

**答案** — less yield (in C than A) AND (as) exothermic (forward reaction)

### (b)(ii) [2]

Describe and explain the change to the rate of the forward reaction that occurs to establish the
equilibrium in C compared to A. You do not need to refer to the Boltzmann distribution in your
answer.

**答案**（increase in temperature / energy from A to C, so）
- M1 more collisions with particles that have *E* ⩾ *E*ₐ AND increase frequency of effective
  collisions
- M2 (initial) rate (of the forward reaction) increases

### (c)(i) [2]

Write an expression for the equilibrium constant, *K*ₚ, for reaction 1. State the units.

**答案**
- M1 *K*ₚ = (*p*NH₃)² / [(*p*N₂)(*p*H₂)³]
- M2 units = atm⁻²

### (c)(ii) [2]

Equilibrium mixture D is made when 1.0 mol of N₂(g) and 3.0 mol of H₂(g) are added to a sealed
container at 750 °C and 1000 atm and left to reach equilibrium. This mixture contains 1.16 mol
of NH₃(g).

Calculate the mole fraction of NH₃(g) in D.

**答案**
- M1 correct amount N₂ at equilibrium = 1 – 1.16 / 2 = 0.42 AND correct amount H₂ at equilibrium
  = 3.0 – (1.16 × 3 / 2) = 1.26
- M2 express amount of ammonia as a mol fraction in terms of total amount of N₂ + H₂ + NH₃ at
  equilibrium = 1.16 / (N₂ + H₂ + 1.16) = 0.408 OR 0.41

### (c)(iii) [1]

The mole fraction of N₂(g) is 0.625 in a new equilibrium mixture, E. Calculate the partial
pressure of N₂(g) in E when the total pressure is 1000 atm.

**答案** — 0.625 × 1000 = 625 atm

When oxides of nitrogen escape into the atmosphere they may be involved in:
- formation of acid rain from sulfur dioxide
- formation of photochemical smog

### (d)(i) [3]

Identify the role of NO and NO₂ in the formation of H₂SO₄ from SO₂ in the atmosphere to produce
acid rain. Use relevant equations to support your answer.

**答案**
- M1 catalyst
- M2 2 equations to show the catalytic activity of NO₂ in formation of SO₃ from SO₂:
  SO₂ + NO₂ → NO + SO₃　　2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂
- M3 describe production of H₂SO₄ from SO₃ and water: SO₃ then reacts with (rain)water /
  moisture / water vapour to make sulfuric acid / H₂SO₄ OR SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄

### (d)(ii) [2]

Outline how NO and NO₂ may contribute to the formation of photochemical smog.

**答案**
- M1 unburned hydrocarbons combine with NO and/or NO₂
- M2 to form PAN / peroxyac(et)yl nitrate
