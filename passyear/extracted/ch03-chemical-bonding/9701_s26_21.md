### (b)(iii) [3] · ch1

Explain the difference in the values of first ionisation energy of sulfur and chlorine in Table 1.1.

**答案**（两条路线任选其一）
- M1 (small) increase in shielding in Cl is outweighed by
- M2 increased nuclear charge in Cl
- M3 Cl has greater nuclear attraction　(ora)

或

- M1 similar shielding
- M2 Cl has greater nuclear charge
- M3 Cl has greater nuclear attraction　(ora)

**判分**
- 三分全给的前提：`chlorine` 在 M1/M2/M3 里**至少点名一次**（名称、符号，或"across the period
  protons 增加"这类趋势表述都算）
- M1：great(er) = increase = high(er) = more = large(r)；shielding 的 similar = constant = equal = same；
  shielding = screening。**I** "从同一 (sub) shell 移走电子"／"shell 数相同"／"electron cloud 相同"／
  轨道电子排布／atomic radius 相关表述
- M2：A 用 number of protons 代替 nuclear charge；A greater atomic number；A "Cl 比 S 多一个 proton"。
  **I** effective nuclear charge 里的 "effective"；**I** nucleon number
- M3：(nuclear) attraction = pull = draws (into nucleus)。M2 与 M3 里 "nuclear" 只需出现一次
- **max 1**：若 M2、M3 都没拿到，但把 greater nuclear attraction 和 greater nuclear charge 关联上了，
  而物种没点名或点错，只给 1 分

### (e) [3] · ch2

Compound X contains sulfur and chlorine only. 100.0 g of X contains 47.49 g of sulfur.
The relative molecular mass, *M*ᵣ, of X is 135.2. Determine the molecular formula of X.
Show your working.

**答案**
- M1 mass Cl = 52.51
- M2 47.49 / 32.1 : 52.51 / 35.5 (= 1.48 : 1.48) → (empirical formula =) SCl
- M3 (*M*ᵣ(SCl) = 67.6，135.2 / 67.6 = 2) molecular formula = S₂Cl₂

**判分** — check working；M1 允许写 100 − 47.49 而不算出结果；
A 其他解法；M2 A ecf from an incorrect M1；M3 **A 只写 S₂Cl₂ 不给过程也算对**
