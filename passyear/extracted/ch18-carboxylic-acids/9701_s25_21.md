## Q5 [12]

Compound A contains the elements carbon, hydrogen and oxygen only.
When A is heated with H₂SO₄(aq), compounds B and C are produced, as shown in Figure 5.1.

![Figure 5.1](img/9701_s25_21_fig5-1.png)

### (a) [2]

Draw the structure of A.

**答案**
- M1 two ester links
- M2 rest of structure correct

A 为 diethyl propanedioate：CH₃CH₂–O–CO–CH₂–CO–O–CH₂CH₃
（即 C₂H₅OOC–CH₂–COOC₂H₅）——中间 CH₂ 两侧各连一个 ester link，两端各为 ethyl group。

### (b) [1]

Name the type of reaction that occurs when A is heated with H₂SO₄(aq).

**答案** — (acid) hydrolysis

### (c) When aqueous Na₂CO₃ is added to separate samples of B and C, effervescence is observed with B only.

### (c)(i) [1]

Complete the equation to describe the reaction between B and an excess of aqueous Na₂CO₃.

**答案** — HOOCCH₂COOH + Na₂CO₃ → NaOOCCH₂COONa + CO₂ + H₂O

### (c)(ii) [1]

Suggest why C does not react with aqueous Na₂CO₃ in a similar type of reaction to B.

**答案** — C is not as strong an acid as B

### (d) A student suggests a two-step synthesis to produce B, as shown in Figure 5.2.

![Figure 5.2](img/9701_s25_21_fig5-2.png)

### (d)(i) [2]

Identify the reagents and conditions required in steps 1 and 2.

**答案**
- step 1 — NaOH(aq) **AND** heat
- step 2 — acidified **AND** potassium dichromate(VI) **AND** heat under reflux

### (d)(ii) [1]

Identify the type of reaction that HOCH₂CH₂CH₂OH undergoes in step 2.

**答案** — oxidation

### (e) B reacts with an excess of reducing agent R to produce D. An excess of PCl₅ is added to D.

- A vigorous reaction occurs.
- Misty fumes are seen.
- Organic compound E is produced.

### (e)(i) [1]

Identify the formula of the reducing agent R.

**答案** — LiAlH₄

### (e)(ii) [2]

Complete the equation to describe the reaction of B with an excess of R.
Use [H] to represent one atom of hydrogen from R.

**答案**
- M1 correct unambiguous organic product
- M2 correct balanced equation
- HOOCCH₂COOH + 8[H] → HOCH₂CH₂CH₂OH + 2H₂O

### (e)(iii) [1]

Name organic compound E.

**答案** — 1,3-dichloropropane
