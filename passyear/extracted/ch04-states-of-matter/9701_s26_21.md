The Contact process uses sulfur to produce sulfuric acid.

stage 1　Sulfur is burned in excess air.

stage 2　2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g)　　Δ*H* = –196 kJ mol⁻¹

stage 3　SO₃ reacts to produce H₂SO₄.

### (a) [1] · ch7

Write an overall equation for stage 3.

**答案** — SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄

**判分** — BOD SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇；BOD H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄

### (b)(i) [1] · ch7

Stage 2 is repeated using a ratio of more than 1 mol SO₂ to 1 mol O₂.

Predict the effect on the percentage yield of SO₃ at equilibrium. Use Le Chatelier's Principle
to explain your answer.

**答案** — increases yield AND equilibrium shifts to right (hand side)

**判分**
- A 替代 increased yield 的说法 = increased amount / more product / SO₃
- A equilibrium shifts to the side with fewer gaseous molecules / to side with fewer gaseous moles
- A equilibrium shifts to the right to reduce the concentration of SO₂
- BOD equilibrium shifts to (favour) forwards reaction/direction
- **I** equilibrium shifts to side with lower volume；**I** limiting reagent；**I** any comments on
  rate；**R** equilibrium shifts to side with more gaseous moles
