## Q2 [9]

Group 18 elements exist as colourless gases at room temperature and pressure.

Table 2.1

| atom | relative volume of atom |
|------|--------------------------|
| neon | 172 |
| argon | 287 |
| krypton | 355 |
| xenon | 446 |

### (a) [2]

Use Table 2.1 to describe and explain the variation in atomic radius down Group 18.

**答案**
- M1 (down the group) (atomic radius) increases OR (atom) volume increases
  OR Ne < Ar < Kr < Xe
- M2 (down the group) the number of shells (of electrons) increases

**判分** — shells = energy levels；**I** number of sub-shells increases；
**I** nuclear attraction of outer electron decreases；
**I** increased shielding effect outweighs the increase in nuclear charge；
**I** outer electron further way

### (b) [3]

Identify **one** Group 18 element from Table 2.1 that behaves most like an ideal gas under
standard conditions. Explain your answer.

**答案**
- M1 neon / Ne (most like an ideal gas)
- M2 it has the smallest volume
- M3 weakest vdw / temporary dipole – induced dipole attractions / London (dispersion) forces /
  London forces

**判分** — M2 与 M3 都需要出现一次比较词（comparative term），只需一次，
例如 "Ne has smaller volume and weak vdw"；M2 BOD "it is the smallest (atom)"；
**I** mass 作为 M2；BOD molecules 代替 atoms；intermolecular forces / IMF's = vdw；
M3 BOD least 代替 weakest；M3 A "forces of attraction between atoms"；
**I** smallest number of electrons

### (c) [4]

A sample of 1.775 g of gas X occupies 617.0 cm³ at 300 K and 101 kPa.
Calculate the relative molecular mass, *M*ᵣ, of X assuming that X behaves as an ideal gas.

*M*ᵣ of X = ……………………

**答案**
- M1 *pV* = *nRT*
- M2 use of appropriate pressure value with volume
  e.g. convert volume to m³ and kPa to Pa OR 101 × 617.0 × 10⁻³ etc.
- M3 calculate value for *n*
  *n* = (101 000 × 617.0 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ 8.31 × 300 = 0.024997 / 0.025
- M4 (1.775 / *M*ᵣ(X) = 0.02499 / 0.025)
  *M*ᵣ = 71(.0)

**判分** — Final answer 71(.0) 四分全给；M1 **I** subsequent incorrect rearrangement；
M2 A 由体积与压强**一致换算**得出的计算过程即可给分，如 101 与 617(.0) × 10⁻³
（**I** 换算单位是否写对，只要提到即可）；
M3 & M4 A ecf from incorrect conversion of volume and/or pressure units；
M3 A ecf from transcription errors / incorrect *T* using correct manipulation of *pV* = *nRT*；
M4 A ecf from number of moles calculated；M4 A ecf from M3；**I** units
