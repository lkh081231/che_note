## Q3 [8]

Separate samples of NaCl and NaBr are reacted with concentrated H₂SO₄. Table 3.1 shows the observations for each reaction.

**Table 3.1**

| reaction | compound | observations |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NaCl | effervescence; steamy fumes that turn blue litmus paper red |
| 2 | NaBr | effervescence; steamy fumes initially that turn blue litmus paper red; brown vapour; pungent smelling gas |

### (a)(i) [1]

Write an equation for reaction 1.

**答案** — H₂SO₄ + NaCl → NaHSO₄ + HCl
　　OR — H₂SO₄ + 2NaCl → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl
**判分** — A Cl⁻ + H₂SO₄ → HCl + HSO₄⁻；A 2Cl⁻ + H₂SO₄ → 2HCl + SO₄²⁻

### (a)(ii) [2]

Explain why reaction 1 is described as a Brønsted–Lowry acid–base reaction.

**答案**
- M1 H₂SO₄ acid **AND** Cl⁻ base
- M2 H₂SO₄ donates proton(s) **AND** Cl⁻ accepts protons　OR H₂SO₄ gives / transfers protons to Cl⁻

**判分** — proton = hydrogen ion；**I** general definition of BL acids and bases；Cl⁻ = Cl in NaCl；**I** "HCl is base / protons given to HCl"

### (b) [3]

Identify the formula of each species responsible for the observations in reaction 2.

**答案**
- M1 (steamy fumes) HBr
- M2 (brown vapour) Br₂
- M3 (pungent gas) SO₂

**判分** — 若给了化学式，**I** 名称；若没给化学式，名称第一次 **I**，之后 A 正确名称；**I** state symbols；M1 **I** HCl；M2 **I** Br；M3 **I** Br₂ and/or HBr

### (c) [1]

Explain why reaction 2 produces brown vapour.

**答案** — (brown vapour is made when) HBr / bromide (anions) are oxidised (by H₂SO₄)
**判分** — A H₂SO₄ / S (in H₂SO₄) is reduced by HBr / Br⁻ / Br in NaBr / Br in HBr；A bromide / Br⁻ loses electrons；A Br⁻ oxidised by S(VI) (in H₂SO₄)；**I** displacement / oxidation products / oxidation numbers（除非明确指 S(VI) in H₂SO₄）；**R** bromine is oxidised by sulfuric acid

### (d) [1]

Explain why a gas with a pungent smell is not produced in reaction 1 but is produced in reaction 2.

**答案** — H₂SO₄ is not (a) strong enough (oxidising agent) to oxidise Cl⁻ / NaCl / HCl
OR Cl⁻ is not (a) strong enough (reducing agent) to reduce H₂SO₄ / S (in sulfuric acid)

**判分** — A HBr / Br⁻ is a stronger reducing agent than HCl / Cl⁻；A reducing ability of halides increases down the group；**I** bond energy / reactivity（未联系 redox）/ electronegativity 论证；**I** reduction product e.g. H₂S；**R** "HCl / Cl⁻ / NaCl is a stronger oxidising agent than H₂SO₄" 这类倒反表述
