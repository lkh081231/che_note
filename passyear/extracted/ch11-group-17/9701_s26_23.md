### (a) The chlorides of Period 3 elements show different physical and chemical properties.

### (a)(i) [3] · ch9

Complete Table 1.1.

| element | formula of chloride | type of bonding in the chloride | type of reaction, if any, of the chloride with cold water |
|---|---|---|---|
| sodium | | | |
| silicon | | | |

**答案**

| element | formula of chloride | type of bonding in the chloride | type of reaction, if any, of the chloride with cold water |
|---|---|---|---|
| sodium | NaCl | ionic | no reaction / none（也接受 dissolves / dissociates / dissolution） |
| silicon | SiCl₄ | covalent | hydrolysis |

**判分** — bonding 一栏若答 giant / molecular 不给分（应是 ionic / covalent）；silicon 一行的
反应类型不接受 hydration，只接受 hydrolysis

### (a)(ii) [1] · ch9

Write an equation for the reaction of PCl₅ with an excess of cold water.

**答案** — PCl₅ + 4H₂O → H₃PO₄ + 5HCl

**判分** — 不接受只水解到 PCl₅ + H₂O → POCl₃ + 2HCl 这一步（题目明确 excess of cold water，
应完全水解到 H₃PO₄）

### (b) The reaction of chlorine with cold water is used in water purification.

### (b)(i) [1] · ch11

Write an equation for the reaction of chlorine with cold water.

**答案** — Cl₂ + H₂O → HCl + HOCl

**判分** — 也接受 Cl₂ + H₂O → 2H⁺ + Cl⁻ + OCl⁻；HOCl 即 chloric(I) acid / hypochlorous acid

### (b)(ii) [1] · ch11

Explain the use of chlorine in water purification.

**答案** — (HOCl **AND/OR** ClO⁻) kill bacteria

**判分** — 不接受只说 chlorine / Cl₂ 本身杀菌，必须点出 HOCl 或 ClO⁻（chlorate(I)）；若 1(b)(i)
方程写对，也接受该反应产物杀菌的 ecf 表述；bacteria 可替换为 microbes / microorganisms /
pathogens，但不接受 viruses

### (c) The relative strength of Cl₂, Br₂ and I₂ as oxidising agents can be compared by adding NaBr(aq) to separate samples of Cl₂ and I₂.

### (c)(i) [1] · ch11

Describe the relative reactivity of Cl₂, Br₂ and I₂ as oxidising agents.

**答案** — Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂ **OR** oxidising power decreases down the group

**判分** — 只写相对反应活性即可，不需要解释原因（写了详细解释也不扣分但不额外给分）

### (c)(ii) [3] · ch11

Complete Table 1.2 to describe the reactions of NaBr(aq) with separate samples of Cl₂(aq)
and I₂(aq).

| aqueous halogen | colour of aqueous halogen | appearance after NaBr(aq) is added | halide ions present in the mixture after the reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cl₂(aq) | pale green | | |
| I₂(aq) | red–brown | | |

**答案**

| aqueous halogen | colour of aqueous halogen | appearance after NaBr(aq) is added | halide ions present in the mixture after the reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cl₂(aq) | pale green | orange / brown（也接受单独的 yellow；不接受 red-brown） | Cl⁻ (and Br⁻) |
| I₂(aq) | red–brown | pale(r) (red-brown)（也接受 no change） | Br⁻ |

**判分** — Cl₂ 行与 NaBr 反应后颜色必须与 I₂ 行明显不同的棕色调，例如 Cl₂ 行写 orange、
I₂ 行写 brown 也算通过（BOD 'brown'）
