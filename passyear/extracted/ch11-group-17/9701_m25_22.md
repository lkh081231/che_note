## Q3 [15]

The halogens chlorine, bromine and iodine show trends in chemical and physical properties down the group. Table 3.1 shows some properties of chlorine, bromine and iodine.

### (a)(i) [1]

Complete Table 3.1.

**Table 3.1**

| property | chlorine | bromine | iodine |
|---|---|---|---|
| colour and state at room temperature | green gas | (dark / red-)brown liquid | grey / black solid |
| bond energy / kJ mol⁻¹ | 242 | 193 | 151 |
| electronegativity | 3.0 | 2.8 | 2.5 |
| formula of sodium halide | NaCl | NaBr | NaI |

### (a)(ii) [2]

The bond energy values in Table 3.1 refer to the X—X bond where X is the halogen. Explain the trend in the bond strength of the X—X bond in the halogens.

**答案**
- atoms larger (down the group)
- distance between electron pair and nucleus greater / longer bond length / lesser orbital overlap
- weaker attraction

### (b) [2]

Explain, with the use of an equation, how chlorine, Cl₂, is used in water purification. State the role of the active species produced.

**答案**
- Cl₂ + H₂O → HCl + HOCl
- ClO⁻ / HOCl kills bacteria

### (c) The sodium halides in Table 3.1 also show trends in chemical properties.

### (c)(i) [1]

Identify the sodium halide that reacts with concentrated H₂SO₄ to form H₂S.

**答案** — sodium iodide / NaI

### (c)(ii) [1]

Identify the sodium halide that only undergoes a Brønsted–Lowry acid–base reaction with concentrated H₂SO₄.

**答案** — sodium chloride / NaCl

### (c)(iii) [1]

A student adds a few drops of AgNO₃(aq) to a solution of NaBr(aq). State what is observed.

**答案** — cream(-coloured) / off-white precipitate / solid forms

### (d) Iodine monobromide, IBr, is a dark red solid that melts near room temperature. IBr reacts with propene. The mechanism for this reaction is the same as the mechanism for that of HBr with propene.

### (d)(i) [1]

Identify all the intermolecular forces that exist between molecules of IBr.

**答案** — instantaneous dipole—induced dipole / id—id / London / dispersion (forces) **AND** permanent dipole—permanent dipole / pd—pd (forces)

### (d)(ii) [1]

Name the mechanism involved in the reaction of IBr with propene.

**答案** — electrophilic addition

### (d)(iii) [2]

The reaction of IBr with propene forms two structural isomers. Draw the two structural isomers shown by these molecules.

**答案**（MS 给的是结构式图，文字转录如下）
- 1-bromo-2-iodopropane：CH₂Br–CHI–CH₃
- 2-bromo-1-iodopropane：CH₃–CHBr–CH₂I

### (d)(iv) [1]

Identify the type of structural isomerism shown by the molecules in (d)(iii).

**答案** — position(al) / regio- (isomerism)

### (d)(v) [2]

Explain why the two structural isomers do not form in equal amounts.

**答案**
- secondary (carbo)(cat)ion / intermediate is more stable / [CH₃CHCH₂I]⁺ is preferred
- electrophile approaches with I nearest / I<sup>δ+</sup> / I⁺ attacks first
- I and Br have different electronegativities / IBr is a polar molecule
