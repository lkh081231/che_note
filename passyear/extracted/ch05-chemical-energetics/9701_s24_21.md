## Q4 [6]

### (a) [2]

Define enthalpy change of formation.

**答案**
- M1 (enthalpy change / energy change) when one mole of a compound / substance is formed
- M2 from its elements in their standard states

### (b) Iron is made when iron(III) oxide is heated with carbon monoxide, as shown by reaction 2.

reaction 2　Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

Table 4.1 shows enthalpy change of formation data measured at 298 K and 101 kPa.

**Table 4.1**

| substance | equation | value for $\Delta H^{\ominus}_{\text{f}}$ / kJ mol⁻¹ |
|---|---|---|
| Fe₂O₃ | | −824.2 |
| CO | | −110.5 |
| CO₂ | C(s) + O₂(g) → CO₂(g) | −393.5 |

### (b)(i) [2]

Complete Table 4.1 by adding equations with relevant state symbols to represent:
- standard enthalpy change of formation for Fe₂O₃
- standard enthalpy change of formation for CO.

**答案**
- Fe₂O₃：2Fe(s) + 1½O₂(g) → Fe₂O₃(s)
- CO：C(s) + ½O₂(g) → CO(g)

**判分** — 两条方程各自独立给分；state symbols 须与该方程配套正确

### (b)(ii) [2]

Use the data in Table 4.1 to calculate the enthalpy change of reaction, Δ*H*ᵣ, in kJ mol⁻¹, for
reaction 2. Show your working.

Δ*H*ᵣ = ..................... kJ mol⁻¹ [2]

**答案**
- Δ*H*ᵣ = −Δ*H*<sub>f</sub>(Fe₂O₃) − (3 × −Δ*H*<sub>f</sub>(CO)) + (3 × −Δ*H*<sub>f</sub>(CO₂))
- = −(−824.2) − (3 × −110.5) + (3 × −393.5)
- = **−24.8 kJ mol⁻¹**

**判分**
- evidence of use of correct expression and/or any rearrangement of only these three enthalpy of formation values
- use correct stoichiometry for these values only
- calculate their expression correctly provided the expression uses only addition/subtraction and all 3 values
