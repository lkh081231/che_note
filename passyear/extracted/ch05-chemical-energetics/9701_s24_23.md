### (b) [2]

Water is one of the products in the reaction of B₂O₃ and NH₃, as shown in reaction 2.

reaction 2　B₂O₃ + 2NH₃ → 2BN + 3H₂O

Table 3.1 shows information about the standard enthalpy change of formation, $\Delta H^{\ominus}_{\text{f}}$,
of some substances.

**Table 3.1**

| substance | $\Delta H^{\ominus}_{\text{f}}$ / kJ mol⁻¹ |
|---|---|
| B₂O₃ | −1264 |
| NH₃ | −46 |
| BN | −134 |
| H₂O | −286 |

Calculate the enthalpy change, Δ*H*, for reaction 2 using the data from Table 3.1.

Δ*H* = ..................... kJ mol⁻¹ [2]

**答案**
- Δ*H* = −(−1264) − (2 × −46) + (2 × −134) + (3 × −286) = **(+)230 kJ mol⁻¹**

**判分**
- M1 correct expression in terms of all 4 enthalpy changes of formation AND correct stoichiometry (as described in given equation)
- M2 correct calculation based on figures given
